# Code of conduct

Ambrotype is made by people who like photographs and want a good free tool for them. We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first language isn't English.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give credit, and accept that maintainers sometimes say no to a change.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Where it applies

In this repository, the hub repository, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/shardpelt/Ambrotype/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct", or message @shardpelt on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may warn, remove content, or ban someone from the project, depending on what happened.

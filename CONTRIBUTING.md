# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) (layout, checks before committing, conventions) and [docs/NEXT.md](docs/NEXT.md) (what's being worked on).

## Licence of contributions

- Ambrotype is GPL-3.0-or-later. By sending a change you agree that it is licensed under the same terms (the plugin SDK under MIT OR Apache-2.0), and that you have the right to send it.
- You keep the copyright in your own work; "The Ambrotype contributors" in the copyright line includes you, and the Git history records what you wrote.
- Please sign off your commits (`git commit -s`), which adds a `Signed-off-by:` line. It confirms the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/): that you wrote the change or may submit it under this licence.
- Code from other projects is welcome only under a GPL-compatible licence, with its credit kept in the file and added to [NOTICE](NOTICE). Never Adobe code, profiles, presets or other data files ([docs/legal-and-naming.md](docs/legal-and-naming.md)).

## Looks and plugins

Layouts, themes and plugins go to the hub repo, not here: see [hub/README.md](hub/README.md) and [docs/ai/plugins.md](docs/ai/plugins.md).

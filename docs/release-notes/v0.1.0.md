# Ambrotype 0.1.0: first preview

Ambrotype is a free, open-source photo editor and organiser in the spirit of Lightroom. This is its first release: an early preview, for trying it out and reporting what breaks.

## What's in it

- **Raw editing** (and JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP) with the Develop panels you know: white balance, tone, curves, colour mixer, colour grading, detail, lens corrections, transform and Upright, effects, calibration, film negatives. Edits go to XMP sidecars; the originals are never touched.
- **Masks:** linear, radial, brush, colour and luminance ranges, Sky, and Subject, Background and People masks that run on your computer.
- **Presets:** your Lightroom `.xmp` and `.lrtemplate` presets, with Amount. Plus Auto, Match look and Surprise me.
- **Library:** folders, collections, smart collections, ratings, flags, keywords, compare and survey, import from a card, and moving over from a Lightroom Classic catalogue.
- **More:** spot removal, HDR and panorama merges, LUTs, a collage maker, frames, and export to JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, OpenEXR and JPEG XL.
- **Make it yours:** task bar (Library, Develop, Color, Retouch, Film, Collage), layouts, themes, plugins and whole custom interfaces, shared through the [hub](https://github.com/Ambrotypehq/ambrotype-hub).
- **AI-friendly:** the `amb` command-line tool and `amb mcp` come with every installer, so any AI app or agent can make the same edits (Help → Connect an AI App…).

## Before you install: the warnings

The installers aren't signed yet, so your system warns about them. That's expected.

- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag Ambrotype to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the app. Close that, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Ambrotype.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** no warning. For the AppImage, make it executable first (`chmod +x`).

## Known limits

- **Not yet measured against Lightroom.** Every rendering model is our own, built by black-box measurement and never from Adobe's code or files. Most are not yet fitted to real Lightroom renders, so a preset can look a little different here than in Lightroom.
- Tested by hand mostly on Linux; macOS and Windows have had less use.
- [GAPS.md](https://github.com/shardpelt/Ambrotype/blob/main/docs/GAPS.md) lists everything still approximate or missing.

## Tell us what breaks

The **Feedback** button in the app opens a pre-filled GitHub issue. Raw files that don't open, presets that look wrong, and crashes are the most useful reports.
